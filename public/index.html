    Eagle Creek course features

    Practice facilities

    • Driving range with target greens
    • Putting green
    • Short game area with sand bunkers
    • Professional lessons available

    Amenities and services

    Caddies and golf cart rentals

    • Carts are equipped with GPS and available to rent; caddie service can be requested in advance.
    • Cart rental $25 per round. Caddie fee $40 per nine holes.

    On-course food and beverage

    • Snack bars and a halfway house sit along the route, and beverage carts circulate regularly.

    When is the best time of year to play at Eagle Creek Golf Club?

    Fall and spring are the best seasons to play at Eagle Creek Golf Club. During these months the weather is mild with minimal rain, which makes for near-perfect golfing conditions. Winter is a good option too for anyone chasing year-round play in Florida's warm climate.
